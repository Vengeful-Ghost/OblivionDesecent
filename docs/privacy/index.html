Privacy Policy

Oblivion Descent · Last updated 5 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Oblivion Descent mobile game ("the game", "we") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By creating an account and playing, you agree to it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: the username you choose and a password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Game data: your saved run (floor, health, gold, items, perks, keys), Soul Altar upgrades and Souls, lifetime statistics (floors cleared, kills, play time and similar), Daily Run results, the players you follow, and co-op party information (party code, players in the party, in-run positions and actions).
  • Usage and device data: Firebase Analytics collects basic usage events and device information (such as device model, operating system version and an app instance ID) so we can see how the game is used and fix problems.
  • Advertising data: if you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads (see section 4).

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, save and load your progress, and sync co-op games between players.
  • To show leaderboards, Daily Run rankings and player profiles.
  • To understand how the game is played, balance it and fix bugs.
  • To show optional rewarded ads, which help keep the game free.

3. What other players can see

Your username, statistics, highest floor and Daily Run results are visible to other signed-in players on leaderboards and profiles. Players in your co-op party see your username and your character during the run.

4. Ads and your consent

Ads in the game are optional: they only play when you tap a button such as "Revive here (ad)" or "x2 Souls (ad)". They are provided by Google AdMob. In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy when that option is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. It is processed only by these service providers:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Realtime Database, Analytics) to store accounts and game data. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google AdMob for rewarded ads. Google Privacy Policy.

Enemy, item and boss content is generated in advance by Google Gemini on our own servers. None of your personal data is sent to it. Data may be stored on Google servers outside your country.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. Co-op party data is temporary and is removed when the party or run ends. Daily Run results are kept so past rankings stay accurate. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and game data within 30 days.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Depending on where you live (for example under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law or the EU/UK GDPR), you may also have the right to data portability and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase, protected by access rules so players can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
